## 6. Proposed acceptance criteria for future implementation authorization

These are review gates, **not approvals issued by this document**. A later authorization must identify its exact scope (basic AG, optional correction, validated delta and specific population/specimen profiles). Deferral must be explicit with safe unavailable behavior; it cannot silently weaken confirmed product direction or waive a dependency. No required in-scope row may remain unresolved.

1. **AC-V01 — signed scope and traceability:** C/L and applicable specialists approve intended users/use, age/sample paths, exclusions and a policy-to-VM-row-to-fixture map. Record product-owner directions as APPROVED separately from professional policies. Reconcile D01/D08/D09/D13/D16 and relevant mixed-disorder decisions without implying global ABG approval.
2. **AC-V02 — basic calculation specification:** approve unit/schema/validity/provenance rules and source-specific reporting; keep potassium exclusion, formula/unit disclosure, signed-result handling and no universal interval. Record approved missing/invalid/implausible actions and each result's prerequisites. Basic AG remains independent of albumin availability under the confirmed direction.
3. **AC-V03 — correction specification when included:** C/L approve formula, coefficient/reference, units/conversions, assay/population domain, timing/compatibility and invalid/missing behavior. Side-by-side labels and missing-albumin limitations have reviewed wording. Independent unit-equivalence and abnormal-albumin evidence is signed off.
4. **AC-V04 — delta validation before inclusion:** C/L independently validate selected formulas, names/units, baseline provenance, corrected/uncorrected dependency, eligibility, uncertainty and zero/near-zero/negative safeguards. Approve unavailable-result states. Validate that output remains numerical with disclosed assumptions and never automatically diagnoses/classifies mixed disorders.
5. **AC-V05 — profiles and method evidence:** for every included lab/profile, obtain applicable local verification, current IFU/version, intervals where used, reporting/uncertainty conventions and paired report evidence. Unknown/expired/incompatible profiles must have approved behavior. Different sample/age stages cannot inherit approval.
6. **AC-V06 — independently reviewed truth set:** every in-scope VM row has literal independent math and clinically/lab approved outcomes, including exclusions, missing/invalid transitions, negative/extreme and exact-boundary neighbors. A second evaluator recomputes references without production code. Record disagreements/resolution and evidence limitations; baseline snapshots remain distinct.
7. **AC-V07 — numerical and communication review:** C/L/E approve raw arithmetic/error bounds versus display rounding, signed zero and eligibility comparisons. C/U review formula/units, assumptions, unavailable explanations and limitations in intended-user scenarios. An in-range gap or compensation result must not imply metabolic completeness. No tolerance is inferred from machine epsilon or printed digits.
8. **AC-V08 — implementation authorization record:** only after the preceding applicable gates have documented professional approval and residual-risk acceptance, obtain a separate explicit implementation authorization identifying specification version, feature/profile scope, deferred work and required engineering/browser regression plan. Passing planned tests after implementation will be necessary conformance evidence, not a substitute for these preimplementation approvals.

Release is a later independent gate: complete implementation conformance, required existing/new tests and browser/usability checks under S AC11–AC14, obtain clinical/laboratory residual-risk acceptance and separate D17/D18 release authorization. Implementation authorization would not itself change release status.

Approval record template for each VM row/gate: selected policy or explicit scope exclusion; rationale; source/IFU/lab profile versions; exact parameters and boundaries; evidence/fixture IDs; uncertainty/residual risks; named C/L/specialist/E reviewers as applicable; approval date and durable record link; review/expiry date. Presently no professional approver, approval date or approval link is assigned. **All unresolved clinical/laboratory policies and gates remain PENDING.**
