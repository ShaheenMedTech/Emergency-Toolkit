## Review and closure discipline

Address H01 and H05–H07 known/pending calculation-interpretation issues before patient-care release; assign ownership/source review for H08/H09 in parallel. This ordering is a qualitative planning judgment, not a numerical risk ranking. Mobile/browser success cannot waive any medication/clinical policy blocker.

Each risk record must acquire: named owner; linked requirements, sources and decision IDs; approved control specification; independent verification and clinical validation evidence; remaining limitations; professional residual-risk acceptance or documented scope exclusion; reviewer/date/record link; review/expiry and change triggers. New evidence may split hazards or add risks. No OPEN risk is closed by software PASS alone. Audit complaint/incident/change records against this register after any future authorized deployment; see [SFDA assessment](SFDA-REGULATORY-ASSESSMENT.md).

Approval owner, clinical/pharmacy/laboratory approvers, review date and signatures: **unassigned / PENDING**. No certification or completeness claim is made for this initial register.
